Even a bank doesn't rely on one appraiser's number alone.

Risk teams routinely adjust the financing they'll actually offer against an appraisal, based on their own read of the market — the appraisal is one input, not a guarantee. When you're working with a fixed budget and a set amount of equity, you need a sense of the order of magnitude before you commit to the seller — not after, when a cautious valuation or a bank unwilling to finance the full amount you were counting on can leave you short with a deal already in motion. Pradixium gives you that independent read early, against a government-sourced benchmark, so the surprise doesn't land after you've already signed. This is a second opinion that complements your appraisal process, not a replacement for it.
