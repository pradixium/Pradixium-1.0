Distressed Assets — Lithuania

Real-estate auctions run by Lithuanian bailiffs (antstoliai) and insolvency administrators (nemokumo administratoriai), read live from the official e-auction portal of the State Enterprise Centre of Registers — evarzytynes.lt. Nothing here is a listing we collected or a price we estimated.

A starting price is not a market value. It is set by the bailiff or administrator for the auction. Enforced sales can carry debts, mortgages, tenants or occupants, and are sold as they are. Always read the conditions on the official auction page — and run the property through Pradixium — before you bid.
Government value check. Registrų centras publishes an official average market value (mass valuation, for property tax) for every property. Tap Government value on an auction: Pradixium shows the property's official unique number, copies it, and opens the official search — paste it there and press Ieškoti. Registrų centras answers only people's browsers directly, so Pradixium never shows that value itself.

"Start price / m²" is the official starting price divided by the official area, shown only when one whole property measured in square metres is sold — never for a fractional share (e.g. "1/4 dalis") or a multi-property lot. Pradixium reads only the official unique number and address from an auction page — never contact details or owner names. The government value is Registrų centras' mass valuation for property tax: an official reference point, not a price. Times are Lithuanian local time.
